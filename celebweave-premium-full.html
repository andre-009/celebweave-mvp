Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

How do QR codes work on CelebWeave?

+

Each guest gets a unique QR code. Scan it at your event to instantly check them in. No duplicate scans allowed—perfect fraud prevention!

Can I import guest lists from Excel?

+

Yes! Bulk import up to 5,000 guests via CSV. Just format as: name, email, phone. QR codes auto-generate and invites send automatically.

How does Aso Ebi coordination work?

+

Create your Aso Ebi store, set colors/sizes, and share with guests. They select their preferences and pay securely. Track all orders in your dashboard.

Is my guest data secure?

+

100% secure. We use bank-level encryption, GDPR compliance, and never share your data. You have full control over guest information.

Do I need a QR scanner app?

+

Nope! Just use our web-based scanner on any smartphone. It works in any mobile browser—no app download needed.

Can I see real-time attendance?

+

Yes! Live dashboard shows check-ins as they happen. See attendance stats, no-shows, timeline, and detailed reports—all in real-time.
